## VEYZL — Best Use of VEYZL

Build an AI project, connect the GitHub repo, and run a VEYZL audit on a pull request. Show that findings changed what shipped (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 controls). Access is free for participating teams during the event.

Judging looks for a working AI project, a real check on a PR, evidence the findings changed the build, and a short demo of issue, fix, and why it matters.

Prize: $500 cash and lifetime pro VEYZL for every team member.

## Perceptron

Two tracks, one winner each. Prizes: JBL speakers.

**Perception.** Use Perceptron Mk / Isaac models via the provided API for image and video: object localization, OCR, captioning, tracking, events in video. Fine-tuning is not expected. Judged on creative, impactful use of the model as provided. API access is covered in the Perceptron workshop.

**Robotics.** Pre-trained checkpoint plus an SO-101 arm, both provided. Teams may collect demonstrations and fine-tune. Perceptron hosts fine-tuning and inference. Workshop attendance is required (arm setup, calibration, teleoperation, data collection, fine-tuning, inference). GPU access for non-UW participants was still being confirmed on the organizer page.

Arm logistics from that page: five arms, about 20 teams, up to four teams per arm on a schedule. Interest form (task, approach, whether you will fine-tune) was due Saturday 10:30 AM PT, with notification by 12:00 PM PT and a required workshop 12:30–1:15 PM PT. Do not plan on an arm unless `docs/CONTEXT.md` says the team has a slot.

Judging for robotics: creativity, task difficulty, technical execution, plus a short video of the working demo.

## Photon — Messaging agent on Photon 2.0

Build an agent on [Photon 2.0](https://photon.co/beta) (beta live 2026-10-15, free) with a phone number and at least one channel: iMessage, SMS, RCS, WhatsApp, Telegram, email, or phone calls. Most creative and technically impressive agent wins.

Guidelines: [Hackathon Guidelines for Photon 2.0](https://somethingreat.notion.site/Hackathon-Guidelines-for-Photon-2-0-3f8e443f36bb81f9893fed0d08a4c01d). SDK intro: [photon.codes/docs/spectrum-ts/introduction](https://photon.codes/docs/spectrum-ts/introduction).

Qualification notes from those guidelines:

- Sign up on the Photon dashboard, create a project, add channels. No promo code.
- iMessage is the text flagship. Voice runs on an SMS number (Voice profile, SIP to Retell, ElevenLabs, LiveKit, or Twilio), not on iMessage.
- Calls and iMessage group chat need a dedicated iMessage line, so hackathon voice builds should use SMS.
